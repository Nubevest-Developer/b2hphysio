Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?

Usually not for a private physiotherapy visit. Some funded services require referrals.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that allows easy movement.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and your goals — we'll discuss this with you after your assessment.

How can I book?

Use the online booking link or call the clinic on 0401 294 468.

Where are you located?

B2H Physiotherapy is based in Sunshine, Victoria. Exact address will be confirmed soon.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, EFTPOS, HICAPS, Medicare, TAC and WorkSafe. See Fees & Rebates for details.
