OUR APPROACH

Personalised physiotherapy,
built around you

B2H Physiotherapy helps patients move with greater comfort and confidence. Physiotherapist Lynn Khoo takes an individual approach — listening to your concerns, assessing how you move and discussing treatment options that genuinely fit your goals.

Our mission is simple: help you work towards a healthier and happier life, one session at a time.

OUR STORY

Back to a healthier & happier life

B2H Physiotherapy was founded on a simple belief — that recovery works best when it is personal, practical and paced to your life.

01

Why we exist

Too many people live with pain they think they just have to put up with. We help you understand what is going on and give you a clear plan to move past it.

02

How we work

Every session starts with listening. We assess your movement, identify the root of the issue and tailor a treatment plan that suits your body and your lifestyle.

03

What you’ll gain

Confidence in your movement, strategies you can keep using at home, and a long-term approach to staying active — not just short-term pain relief.

OUR VALUES

What makes B2H different

A calm, honest, evidence-informed space to recover — whether you’re nursing a new injury or managing a long-standing issue.

Patient-first care

Your goals, your pace. Every plan is tailored to the person in the room, not a textbook protocol.

Clear, honest advice

No jargon, no upsell. You’ll always know what’s happening, why, and what the next step looks like.

Movement-focused

We prioritise active rehabilitation — exercises and strategies that build strength and resilience, not passive quick-fixes.

Evidence-informed

Treatment choices are grounded in current best practice and adapted to what actually works for your body.
